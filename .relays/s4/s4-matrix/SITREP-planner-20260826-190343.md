rather than on content — an instrument returning its negative for everything. Run 2 is correctly
invoked and returns its positive for everything. Neither run separates the sets, so **per-file
lint does not explain the omission** and the "these are the malformed 27" story is unconfirmed by
any instrument I hold.

I am reporting the coincidence and stopping there. Confirming it needs the migration's own
inventory, which is master's to produce; `migrate` is not a subcommand I will run without a
